Project

Kenya, a history

A scroll-driven history of Kenya covering its history, 12 community profiles, and culture. Every claim is footnoted to a source, and claims without one are visibly flagged.

HTMLCSSJavaScriptGitHub Pages
KENYA / HISTORY
❖

Overview

Claims in the site are footnoted to numbered sources. A claim that is not yet backed by a source carries a SOURCE PENDING badge instead of being presented as settled.

What it contains

  • 6 chapters that tell the story as you scroll.
  • 12 community profiles.
  • 19 numbered sources.
  • A reference page listing the claims that have a source, the research gaps, and the books I have not yet read.

How it is built

  • Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with no framework.
  • CSS custom properties, including registered properties with @property.
  • Scroll-driven presentation using IntersectionObserver.
  • Keyboard-accessible filters, and reduced-motion support for visitors who ask for it.
  • Hosted on GitHub Pages.

Limits

  • The community profiles are drafts. Members of those communities have not reviewed them yet.
  • Some claims are still marked SOURCE PENDING.
  • There are no photographs yet.

The site is a research project in progress.
